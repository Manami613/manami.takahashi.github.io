Project: Isolation and loneliness prevention network through service mobilitiesプロジェクト: サービス・モビリティを通じた孤立・孤独予防ネットワーク

A three-year project nationally funded by RISTEX, Japan Science and Technology Agency (JST), as a joint initiative among Keio University, Nagoya University, and the University of Tokyo.

  • Co-designed and implemented a census survey on social isolation and loneliness, distributed to 13,000 households in Oiso Town, Kanagawa.
  • Conducted semi-structured interviews with 20 community health professionals — home care nurses, community nurses, social workers, and community welfare commissioners — to investigate factors contributing to moral distress in community health practice.

科学技術振興機構（JST）社会技術研究開発センター（RISTEX）の助成を受けた3年間のプロジェクト。慶應義塾大学・名古屋大学・東京大学による共同研究です。

  • 訪問看護師・地域看護師・ソーシャルワーカー・民生委員など20名の地域保健専門職に半構造化インタビューを行い、地域保健実践における道徳的苦悩の要因を調査。
